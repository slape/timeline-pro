## 01-glossary.md
**Purpose:** Canonical definitions to avoid naming collisions. Keep entries short and unambiguous.

# Glossary
- **TimelineItem**: A scheduled unit with start/end and optional lane.
- **Lane**: Visual row grouping for timeline items.
- **Y-Delta**: The vertical offset applied to a TimelineItem relative to its computed lane.
- **Row/Lane Shift**: Operation that moves items between lanes according to conflict rules.
- **Invariant**: A rule that must always be true.
